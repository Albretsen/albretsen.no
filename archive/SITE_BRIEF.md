# SITE_BRIEF.md

## Purpose

This website is Asgeir's personal landing page as a developer.

It should quickly communicate:
- who he is
- what kind of developer/builder he is
- what he has built
- how to contact him

It should feel personal, competent, modern, and direct.

## Tone

The tone should be:
- honest
- straight to the point
- confident without sounding fake
- technically credible
- personal, but not oversharing

Avoid:
- corporate fluff
- exaggerated self-praise
- vague inspirational nonsense
- overcomplicated storytelling

## Positioning

Default positioning unless Asgeir says otherwise:
- software developer
- builder of practical products and experiments
- someone who values useful software, direct communication, and clean systems

Do not make stronger claims than the evidence supports.

## Audience

Primary audience:
- potential collaborators
- potential employers/clients
- technical people evaluating Asgeir quickly
- people who want a fast sense of what he does

The site should make a strong impression within seconds.

## Visual direction

Aim for:
- clean and sharp
- modern but restrained
- high contrast
- strong spacing and typography
- minimal but not empty
- a premium feel without looking like a template

Avoid:
- gimmicky animations as the main attraction
- clutter
- too many sections
- trying to look like a VC-backed SaaS homepage

## Information architecture

The site should likely include:
1. Hero / introduction
2. Short about / positioning section
3. Selected projects
4. Optional experiments or side work if they help the story
5. Contact / links

Possible future additions if justified:
- work history
- writing
- testimonials
- resume / CV link

Do not add major sections just to fill space.

## Copy rules

Copy should be:
- short
- concrete
- readable
- specific when possible

Prefer plain language over hype.

## Facts and sources

Allowed sources for research:
- existing repo content
- existing site content
- GitHub
- LinkedIn
- other public references

But:
- treat external sources as research, not automatic truth
- if a claim might be outdated, sensitive, or ambiguous, ask before adding it

## Interaction model

This is primarily a fast landing page, not a complex app.

Prioritize:
- readability
- navigation clarity
- mobile usability
- lightweight performance

## Success criteria

The site is succeeding if:
- the hero immediately says who Asgeir is
- the projects section feels credible and interesting
- the overall look feels polished
- the site loads quickly and works well on mobile
- a technical visitor can understand Asgeir quickly and know how to reach him
