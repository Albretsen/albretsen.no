00 · Direction

A cabinet of lattices.

Albretsen AS presented as a natural-history atlas of things that were built: every product is a specimen, catalogued, labelled and lit like an editorial still life. The hero is Specimen No. 1, a brass and glass radiolarian built from nothing but three.js primitives after Haeckel's Circogonia icosahedra (1887). Precision is in the lattice; play is in the springs, the loupe and the eosin-pink beads. Cream, forest and brass, with one stain of seed-hue 336.

01 · Identity

The lattice A.

Two legs, a crossbar and an inner V make an A out of a triangulated lattice: five nodes, one apex stained eosin. It reads as a letter, a molecule and a small network graph. Wordmark: Fraunces 600 at optical size 144, outlined; “AS” in Geist Mono.

02 · Palette

Cream, forest, brass. One stain.

Defined in OKLCH. Eosin (hue 336, the seed) is the histology stain that makes structure visible: use it for one thing per screen, never for large areas. Blue hour swaps the page to forest and ink.

03 · Type

Editorial serif, honest mono.

Fraunces (display, opsz 144, SOFT 50; italics at 300 for asides) · Instrument Sans (text, UI) · Geist Mono (specimen labels, numbers, captions; uppercase, +0.14em). All OFL, all on Google Fonts.

04 · Hero imagery

Specimen No. 1, photographed.

Baked offline from the live three.js scene: 40 jittered passes averaged for a soft area light, lens aperture and anti-aliasing, then graded (forest into the shadows, brass into the highlights), with halation and film grain. Four lights match four phases of the Norwegian day. Each ships at 1× and 2×; day and dusk also as AVIF.

05 · Live 3D

Kept alive.

The same specimen as a self-contained module: 3d/specimen.js. Drag to turn it; a flick keeps its momentum through a hand-rolled spring; when idle it drifts. Its light follows the sun over Oslo. Also exported as standard glTF: specimen.glb (viewer, full demo).

06 · Products

Specimen plates.

Each live or building product is a plate: its real logo, untouched, printed on a cream specimen card with a brass pin, on forest bookcloth. A brass loupe lies on the card and truly magnifies the status stamp (the lens samples the card texture at 1.75×). Drag the top plate away, flick it, or tap it to turn it over.

07 · From the archive

Lattices nobody had to design.

Open-access natural history, graded into the palette: Haeckel's radiolarian engravings (1887–1904) reprinted in forest ink on cream, and Wilson Bentley's snow-crystal photomicrographs from the Smithsonian, split-toned. Use them as plates between sections, in the footer, behind numbers. Hover: the loupe.

08 · Texture & stickers

Paper, cloth, stamps.

Tileable cream paper and forest bookcloth (512px, FFT-generated so they repeat seamlessly), a neutral grain overlay for soft-light blending, a hex lattice pattern, and four stickers with all text outlined.

09 · Motion

Springs, by hand.

One integrator (Spring: semi-implicit Euler, fixed 240 Hz sub-steps) drives everything in motion.js. No library. Each signature is named, tuned and replayable here. Reduced motion collapses them to fades.

01 · Entrance

Settle

From 18px below at scale .965 with a 6px blur, to rest on Spring(170, 26). Staggered 70ms. Never from scale(0).

02 · Hover

Loupe

Tilts up to 7° toward the pointer, lifts 4px, a brass sheen follows the cursor; presses to .97. Spring(320, 24).

03 · Transition

Shuffle

See 06 · Products

Drag, flick past 28% or 650px/s, and the plate springs to the back of the stack with Spring(140, 15). Tap flips it on Spring(210, 20, m 1.1).

04 · Delight

Daylight

NOAA solar position over Oslo sets data-phase (dag, gulltime, blåtime, natt), swaps the hero render and re-lights the live specimen. Updates every minute.
