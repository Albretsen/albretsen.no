      Method

      Small parts, put together with care.

      The organism at the top of this page is Haeckel’s plate, rebuilt from nothing but cylinders and spheres in three.js, photographed over forty exposures and lit by wherever the sun over Oslo is right now.

      It is the same method as the products: a few well-chosen primitives, assembled precisely, kept running long after launch.

      Takes one outside engagement at a time.
