01 · palette

Butter, leaf and air.

Seed hue 96° in OKLCH: high lightness, low-to-medium chroma. Butter is the brand; leaf means "new block"; the cool pastels are room colour, never headline colour. Ink is an olive near-black, not grey.

08 · interactive 3D

Voxels from CSS transforms only.

3d/voxel-css.js builds real voxel models out of divs with preserve-3d, drawing only faces that touch air. Drag to orbit; it springs back to the iso angle. Faces can carry images, which is how the real product logos sit on the shelf cubes.
