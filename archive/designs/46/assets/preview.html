08

Motion

Five signature moves in motion.js (Web Animations API), CSS fallbacks in motion.css. Sounds play here only if you switch sound on.

Iris Open

Entrance. A hexagonal aperture rotates open over the image, over-exposed at first, like a lens stopping down. Inspired by the film iris wipe.

Squish

Hover and press. Clay squash-and-stretch that rebounds on a spring baked into CSS linear(). After Josh Comeau's "boop".

Rack Focus

Section transition. Soft and over-exposed to sharp, hunting once before it locks, with an AF double beep.

Shutter Flash

Click. A white flash over the whole page and a burst of candy sprinkles from the pointer.

Sticker Slap

Entrance for stickers and badges: dropped from above, overshoots, shadow tightens on impact.

Blink

The mascot's own idle: random blinks every 2 to 5 s, gaze follows the pointer, breathing squash. Built into blink-live.js.
