The Vivarium

Asset kit for design 54. A company kept like a terrarium: each product is a creature, alive under glass, catalogued like a naturalist would. Paper and ink, one moss-green spot colour, storybook 3D built from primitives, and 17th-century specimens from The Met's open-access collection.

01 · Identity

A bell jar, one sprout

The mark is a cloche over a two-leaf seedling: something kept, something growing. The right leaf carries the only colour. The wordmark is Fraunces at its largest optical size, outlined, with a moss full stop before a mono AS.

04 · Hero imagery

05 · Live 3D

The Wardian case, CSS only

The centrepiece: a gabled glass case made of DOM planes and CSS 3D transforms. Ferns, moss and the four creatures stand on their own Z planes; the case turns toward the pointer. No canvas. live/diorama.js + diorama.css
