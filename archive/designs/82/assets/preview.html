The company is the release.

Q.E.D. — a product-drop page for a company, not a product. Black, white and one dawn.

00 / Idea

Proofs, written in shapes.

In 1847 Oliver Byrne printed Euclid with the words replaced by coloured shapes. This kit does the same for a software company: every product, fact and button is a glyph built from strokes and one sun, and the glyphs morph into one another the way one proposition leads to the next. The site is staged as a launch page whose product is the company itself (“Drop 00”). Three spark words run through everything: fracture (one break in a stroke, the crack in the monolith), dawn (the single amber primary, always a sector of a circle) and gravity (springs that settle, a mass that bends the grid, an easter egg where everything falls).

01 / Hero

Two renders, one light.

Ray-marched in GLSL (src/render.html), baked in headless Chrome at 3×3 supersampling, graded with film grain. Each ships at 960/1600/2400 wide plus a portrait crop, in AVIF, WebP and JPEG.

02 / Palette

Black, white, one dawn.

Seed hue 72° in OKLCH. Neutrals carry a whisper of the same hue so the amber never looks pasted on. Amber is for the sun only: one sector per glyph, one accent per screen.

03 / Type

Geist, set tight. Geist Mono, set wide.

Both on Google Fonts (SIL OFL). The wordmark is not a font: it is drawn from the icon grammar (monoline, mitred, Λ for A).

04 / Identity

A, fractured, with the sun inside.

The mark is the first glyph of the system: an apex, a horizon for a crossbar, the sun rising on it, and one leg broken and sheared. Triple-click it anywhere for a surprise.

05 / Glyphs

Thirty glyphs, one grammar.

24 grid · 1.5 stroke · butt caps · mitre joins · at most one sun (a filled circle sector) · at most one fracture. Hover for the name (tooltip primitive), click to send it to the morph stage below.

06 / Centrepiece

Proof Morph.

Any glyph becomes any other. Strokes are resampled to 64 points, paired by nearest centroid, and staggered so the glyph is “re-proved” stroke by stroke; mid-flight each stroke shears apart along its own fracture. The sun is never interpolated as a path: its centre, radius and angles tween, with a gravity overshoot on the radius.

Auto-cycle: mark → MCP Emails → Fun Libs → Toolrails → Harbor → Q.E.D.

07 / Products

Each product, a weight on the sheet.

The real logos, unchanged, on black-glass tiles floating over their own gravity well. The four plates are the same scene at four moments of one sunrise (05:10, 05:40, 06:20, 07:00), so a row of them reads as time passing.

Retired: stand-ins in the glyph system (Luma keeps its real monogram)

08 / Extras

Texture and ephemera.

Byrne’s plates regraded into the palette; the company as an orbit diagram; a release label; dividers, paper and grain.
