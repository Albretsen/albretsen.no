    About the town

    Everyone on the promenade keeps a schedule. Inès runs at six, Marcel sets out the chairs just before seven, Henri and Odile take the good bench mid-morning, and at sunset half the town leans on the railing. When someone gets home after dark, their window lights up; when they go to bed, it goes out. Left alone, the town follows the clock in Oslo. Drag the sun and everyone reschedules.

    Built with three.js and a library of low-poly people, buildings and props made for this site. The figures on the shop screens are the products' real numbers.
