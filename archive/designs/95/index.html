      How the town works

      Two controls: the sun and the years.

      • The years run from 2016, when the first app went on Google Play, to today. Each product's building rises in the year it started. Houses and people fill in around them.
      • The sun runs from afternoon to two in the morning. Windows switch on as the light goes, then off again as the town goes to bed. Live products keep their lights on.
      • Click a product to read its card. Click any other house to flick its lights.
      • Keys: ← → for the years, [ ] for the sun, Space to play.
