Art direction

HonestDeck.

Hero ideaEvery product is a card. The deck deals itself out on load, and on scroll it replays as a pre-rendered image sequence. Live ones face up, retired ones stamped FOLDED. Nothing is hidden.
LookRetro-futurist airbrush: 80s chrome, hard horizons, glossy candy spheres, sunset gradients and grain. Candy palette seeded at hue 120°.
VoiceBrutal honesty. Real numbers on every card, including the small ones. The retired pile gets the same care as the winners.

01 · Hero imagery

Rendered, not drawn.

Baked from the same three.js scene that runs live (3d/deck-scene.js): extruded rounded-rect cards, a chrome sphere nodding to NASA's Echo balloon, candy satellites, an echo-ring floor. Each ships as 2560 webp + avif, and a 1280 webp.

02 · Identity

A house of cards that reads as an A.

Two cards lean together, a third lies across them edge-on. It's honest about the risk of building on a stack of bets, and it still holds. Wordmark: Unbounded 800 outlined, with an 80s chrome fill: a hard horizon at 54% and a night drop shadow.

03 · Palette

Candy, seeded at 120°.

Apple green is the seed and the floor. Bubblegum, hot pink and grape make the sky; tangerine and lemon make the horizon. Night and plum are the only darks. Defined in OKLCH; hex for tools that need it.

06 · Photography

Signal and echo, 1960s.

Two public-domain NASA photographs, gradient-mapped into the palette. Project Echo was a 30-metre aluminised balloon that bounced radio signals across a continent: a chrome sphere that did one thing well. The Holmdel horn picked up the faint echo of the Big Bang. Credit NASA.

09 · Motion study

Deal, tilt, riffle.

motion.js uses Motion 12.23.28 (motion.dev); motion.css holds the CSS-only pieces. Springs throughout, nothing linear except the scrub. Everything stops under prefers-reduced-motion.

Deal · entrance

Cards leave a squared deck on a spring (stiffness 170, damping 19), flip face up mid-air, 75 ms stagger from the centre.

Airbrush tilt · hover

Card tilts toward the pointer and a hard specular highlight follows it, like light on a chrome bumper. After Magic UI's Magic Card.

Riffle · transition

Two half-decks bridge and interleave. Content swaps at the bridge. Use it for filters and page changes.
