Design 36 · Asset kit · Infrared Fable

A real landscape, shot in light you can't see, with a storybook living inside it.

False-colour infrared photographs (CC0 Poly Haven HDRIs, regraded in the Aerochrome manner: foliage glows magenta, skies fall to violet and cyan) carry the type inside the scene. Hand-coloured 1830s natural-history plates and Grandville's 1844 Un autre monde are cut out and re-inked into the same infrared palette, so engraving and photograph read as one world. Everything technical stays crisp; everything around it is warm, odd and alive.

01

Palette — infrared, seed hue 192°

Three hot inks (magenta, ember, peach) burn against two night violets. The single cool note is the horizon cyan at the seed hue; use it once per view, like the strip of sky under the trees.

04

Hero imagery

Two infrared plates, each with a matching front layer (same photograph, sky cut away) so type can be set between the sky and the trees. Shipped as AVIF 1440/2880 + WebP 1440. Stack: back plate → headline → front plate.

05

Product plates

Each live or building product is a numbered specimen plate: the real logo, unaltered, pinned on a cream mount inside the arch window, over a scene from the kit. Plates are also the faces of the interactive specimen deck below.

Interactive · interactive/specimen-deck.js

Drag it. Fling it. Flip it.

A dependency-free DOM stack with real spring physics: the top plate lags the pointer with weight, tilts with tension, flies past the horizon when flung and springs back to the bottom of the pile. Tap to flip for the facts. Keyboard: Enter flips, arrows shuffle.

07

Motion study — motion.css + motion.js (GSAP 3.12.5 + ScrollTrigger)

Five named signatures. Everything degrades to CSS and respects reduced motion.

Toolrails

Horizon Rise entrance

Letters climb out from behind the trees with blur and a centre-out stagger (expo.out, 1.6s); the scene's three depth layers then parallax at different rates on scroll.

Wingbeat idle · hover

Any butterfly image is split into two halves hinged at the body and flapped with rotateY in 3D. Idle beats arrive at random intervals; hovering makes it flutter.

Magnetic Read the notes

Links get an ink thread that runs like a horizon.

Magnetic + Ink Thread hover

Buttons lean toward the pointer within a 90px field (gsap.quickTo), the label leans further than the shell, and release snaps back on elastic.out(1, .32). Links draw a moving magenta wave underline.

Ember Drift ambient

A six-second breathing glow (drop-shadow + saturation) for the mark, the sun and key figures. Cheap, CSS-only, and the only thing allowed to loop forever.
