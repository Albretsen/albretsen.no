Cloud Chamber

Asset kit for albretsen.no. Liquid chrome and iridescent glass, set in a calm rose laboratory. Letters are physical objects: they fall, collide, and every impact leaves a bubble-chamber track. Every pixel here is made, simulated or museum-sourced; nothing is stock.

01

Direction

A cloud chamber makes the invisible legible: a particle passes, the gas condenses, and a precise spiral appears. That is the site's job for Asgeir’s work. The brand looks like a quiet instrument with chrome objects in it, never like a bank or a consultancy.

Hero
A physics playground. ALBRETSEN in chrome falls into the chamber and can be grabbed and thrown. Each hard impact draws spiralling tracks that fade slowly.
Materials
Mirror chrome, rose-tinted metal, and thin-film iridescence. Next to them sit real 1,800-year-old Roman and Islamic glass whose iridescence comes from weathering.
Personality
Scientific calm. Long, even easing; one overshoot at most; labels in mono; numbers set like measurements.
Signature
Decode: text resolves out of a churn of brand glyphs, like a reading settling.
Avoid
Gears, ships, timetables, split-flaps, finance cues. Glow, neon, and generic blob gradients.
02

Palette: one hue, twelve depths

Every colour sits on OKLCH hue 348°. The rose ramp handles surfaces and type. The low-chroma steel ramp is what the chrome reflects, so the metal reads as silver rather than as pink plastic. Product logos keep their own colours.

04

Identity

The mark is Unbounded’s A, cut along one slanted line, with the upper shard slightly displaced. The break is the same line used by the Fracture page transition. Every logo and wordmark below is a pure vector built from the font’s real outlines.

05

Hero imagery

All of these are three.js renders. Letters and primitives were dropped with cannon-es, and the frame was rendered once they came to rest. They ship at 2880 px for retina and 1440 px, as WebP plus AVIF for the two lead images, with 9:16 crops for phones.

06

Live: chamber + specimen

chamber.js is the hero prototype. It uses canvas 2D and matter.js 0.20, draws the baked chrome sprites, generates particle tracks on impact, and responds to drag, throw and click. specimen.js is the logo in live three.js: drag to turn it, hover to close the fracture. Both pages are in 3d/demo.html.

07

Products, mounted as specimens

Each real logo from designs/_shared/products sits unaltered as a texture on a chrome-backed tile in a wine dish. Harbor is shown in the colourway of its own banner. Retired products without assets get a single chrome specimen each; Luma keeps its real wordmark.

08

Museum plates

These are CC0 open-access photographs from The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Ancient glass gains its iridescence through centuries of weathering, a slow counterpart to the chrome renders. Each photo is graded into the rose ramp while the original colour is kept riding on top.

09

Extras

These give the site texture: baked track plates for section backgrounds, chrome numerals and a year object, a sticker built from the font outlines, and a tileable graticule.

10

Motion study

The timing is in motion.css and the behaviour in motion.js, built on GSAP 3.13 with ScrollTrigger. Every animation honours prefers-reduced-motion.
