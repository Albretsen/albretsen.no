Design 38 · asset kit · art direction

Salon Refusé after hours

A gallery wall at night, lit like a museum. Every framed work is a canvas-2D program that paints itself; the products hang in the same light as the art. Put a UV torch to any frame and you see the underdrawing: the vector field, the catenaries, the construction grid. The craft is in the hidden layer. Everything on this sheet was rendered, generated or measured, not drawn by hand.

Room 03

Palette

Jade wall from seed hue 168°. Full spectrum, with discipline: six hues 60° apart at one lightness and chroma. A work or a section uses its home hue, its opposite and neutrals. Only the brand spark gets the whole spectrum. UV inks belong to the hidden layer and nowhere else. Hover a swatch for hex.

Room 09

Motion study

motion.js (Web Animations API) + motion.css. Click a card to replay it.

01 Lights up · entrance. Lamp warm-up flicker on the pool, then a top-down clip-path: inset() unveil, 1000 ms cubic-bezier(.77,0,.175,1) after Emil Kowalski.
02 Swing · hover. Keyframes sampled from a damped pendulum (g/L, e−2.4t), rotating about the nail. Hover side sets direction.
03 Rehang · transition. Lift off the wall (shadow grows, fades), drop the next work on the nail, settle with a swing.
04 Red dot · detail. The gallery "sold" sticker for products earning money. From scale .9 with 2px blur, overshoot 1.08, settle; 380 ms.
05 Press · tactile. Scale .97 on pointerdown, 120 ms ease-out; release 200 ms.
06 UV torch · signature hover. Registered @property --torch-r animated by WAAPI; pointer position lerped each frame; radial mask reveals the underdrawing.
