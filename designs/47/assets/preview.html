Two ways in.

Stille.
A quiet archive
of working things.

Daylight on a plaster wall, one shelf, and the products set out like objects in a museum store: catalogued, lit, given room. Paper and ink, Hammershøi greys from the Statens Museum for Kunst, and one colour that means "this one is mine": aniline violet, the first synthetic dye. The shelf is built from primitives in three.js, baked to photographs, and kept live.

03 / Identity

An A that is a shelf.

Two legs make a gable; the crossbar runs past them like a board on a wall; one violet square is kept on it. The wordmark is Instrument Serif, outlined, with AS in Geist Mono set in the spot colour.

06 / Products

The collection.

Each live product photographed as an object on the shelf, its real icon untouched, with a string tag from the catalogue. Click one: the Vitrine transition.

07 / Retired

Plaster casts.

Retired products without surviving brands become unglazed plaster tiles: same shape, no colour, a debossed glyph. Honest stand-ins, clearly not logos. Luma keeps its real wordmark.

08 / Plates

From the archive.

Public-domain scans from SMK, the National Gallery of Denmark (Public Domain Mark). Two kinds: honest colour scans, and ink prints in the brand's paper and ink. Use them as section breaks, behind quotes, beside long text.

09 / Extras

Light, grain, tags, one cube.

Small things that make the page feel like a room: a window's light to lay over any section, a film-grain tile, catalogue tags, and the violet cube as a loose object.

Movement that keeps its voice down.
