Asset kit No. 052 · Violet Cabinet

A cabinet of working software.

Violet Cabinet treats the company as a museum archive that has been plugged into the mains. Real public-domain plates (Kurr's Mineral Kingdom, 1859; Haeckel's Kunstformen der Natur; Benjamin West's Franklin) are scanned, cut out and electrified: paper becomes saturated violet, engraved lines become light, and every highlight carries a single spark of yellow.

The site reads as a catalogue: chapters are Tab. I, Tab. II…, figures are numbered, and facts sit on specimen tags. The precision is real (the mark is a Japan-law quartz twin at exactly 84°33′); the play is in how things move: crystals morph, a bust gets scanned, buttons lean toward the cursor.

Scroll-driven chapters · SVG morphing · Lenis · magnetic links

Identity

Tab. A.

The mark is two tabular quartz crystals mitred on a vertical twin plane at 84°33′, the real angle of a Japan-law twin. Together they form an A; the crossbar is the current between them, in spark yellow. The wordmark is Bodoni Moda italic (hairlines like crystal edges) with a Geist Mono suffix.

Centrepiece · SVG morph

Tab. IX.

svg/crystal-morph.js scrubbed by scroll. Each chapter is a crystal habit from Kurr's Plate A; the outline is one path resampled to 120 points, facet edges redraw with stroke-dashoffset and hidden edges stay dotted like the engraving. The last chapter resolves into the mark.

Tab. III.

Motion study

Tab. XII.

motion.css + motion.js. Lenis smooths the scroll; CSS animation-timeline: view() and scroll() drive entrances with no JS; a JS fallback toggles .is-in. All of it is off under prefers-reduced-motion.

01 · entrance

Plate develop

A scan "develops" out of a hexagonal crystal section: bleached and blurred to full colour as it crosses the viewport. After NRK's scroll-driven title cards.

02 · entrance

Scan reveal

A spark-yellow scan line passes over the image and leaves it behind, the 2D twin of the scan bust. Registered @property --scan on a view timeline.

03 · hover

Magnetic facet

Buttons and links lean toward the pointer from 1.6× their size; the label travels further than the shell, and a facet of spark follows the cursor. Springs back with overshoot.
