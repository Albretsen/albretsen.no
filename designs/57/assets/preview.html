Art direction

Weightless, rebellious, exact.

Escape Vector treats the public-domain archive of spaceflight like an indie zine treats a photocopier. NASA and ESA photographs are pushed to black and white, and only one thing ever gets colour: re-entry red, OKLCH hue 24. The Earth in Earthrise, the stripes on McCandless's suit, the probe on the terrain, the tip of the A.

Around the photographs sit 19th-century engravings (Trouvelot's astronomical drawings, Haeckel's radiolarians from the Biodiversity Heritage Library) and hand-authored vector annotation: trajectories, registration marks, a probe that reads altitude. The tone is a solo builder who left orbit on purpose.

07 Motion

Springs, not curves.

motion.js: one 4 kB module, a damped oscillator integrated at 240 Hz, and a shared rAF loop that sleeps when everything rests.

Shipping since 2016.

08 CSS 3D

Twenty triangles, no WebGL.

radiolarian.js: an icosahedron of DOM faces placed by matrix3d. Throw it, it keeps your spin. Click it, it comes apart along its normals and springs back.
