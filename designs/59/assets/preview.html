Asset kit 59 Art direction · Cover Story

A cover you can open.

The site is the current issue of a magazine about one company. Editorial gravitas in the type and photography; neo-brutalist stickers carry the facts. Open the cover and the stickers scatter, the page turns on its spine, and a crystal rises out of the spread.

01 Centrepiece · three.js

The cover, in three dimensions.

three/cover-scene.js. Tilt with the pointer (or the phone), drag or click the cover to open it, Enter on keyboard. A CC0 magnifying glass from Poly Haven follows the pointer and genuinely magnifies the cover print. Loaded lazily when this section scrolls into view.

Tilt. Then open the cover.

02 Hero imagery

Covers, spreads and renders.

03 Identity

A sticker that peels.

The mark is a violet sticker whose corner is peeling back to show its lime underside: the cover opening, in one gesture. All glyphs are outlines; no font is needed to render any identity file.

04 Palette · seed hue 288°

Full spectrum, one at a time.

Ink, paper and violet do the work. The spectrum lives only on stickers, and each sticker gets exactly one hue. The only place several hues meet is light through a crystal (the photography, the 3D dispersion, the Glint motion).

Approximate share of any screen: paper 52, ink 26, violet 12, glow 3, the five sticker hues 7 together.

09 Motion study · motion.css + motion.js

Six motions, all physical.

Easing: slap cubic-bezier(.2, 1.6, .35, 1) · lift cubic-bezier(.3, .7, .2, 1) · page cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .25, 1) · tilt spring k 120, critically damped. Every motion falls back to a fade under prefers-reduced-motion.
