03 — Identity

A doorway with the sun on its sill.

The mark is the house’s arch in falu red with a low sun resting on the threshold. The wordmark is Albert Sans Light, outlined, with “AS” set small and tracked.

04 — Palette

Small‑town Norway, in low sun.

White clapboard, falu red, ochre and slate do the work. Sky, horizon and bougainvillea appear only inside images and on hover.

06 — Extras

Texture and small life.

Tileable clapboard and whitewash, a bougainvillea cut‑out for hover edges, a low‑sun vignette for section breaks, folio furniture, and Norway from orbit as a quiet plate.

07 — Motion

Slow, unbroken, never bouncy.

Five named studies in motion.css / motion.js. Hover and scroll to play them.

08 — Interactive

The set, live.

3d/terrace.js renders the miniature in three.js (r169) with soft shadows, ambient occlusion, tilt‑shift and a film grade. Scroll inside the frame: one camera move, from the spread to the lit doorway. It loads only when it comes into view.
