Totality, an archive of light

Direction

The company is presented like a set of astronomical plates in a museum drawer: each product is an observation, mounted on corona-pink paper, captioned in quiet caps. The imagery is É. L. Trouvelot's 1870s chromolithographs (public domain), re-inked as a duotone on hue 324: umbra ink and corona light. One idea per screen, a lot of dark space, and one moment of glow. The page opens on an eclipse and the moon is the aperture everything is seen through.

Identity, the diamond ring

Mark · wordmark · favicon · OG

The mark is the moment just before totality: the moon almost covers the sun, the corona ring is broken by an aperture gap, and a single bead of light sits in it. It reads at 16 px as a dot in a ring.
