# Living Town: where the sounds come from

Almost every sound in the town is made in the visitor's browser by the recipes in
`site/static/assets/town/js/sound/recipes-*.js` (sea, breeze, birds, cicadas, crickets, the fountain,
footsteps, cups, the bicycle bell, the lamps, the chime, the church bell, the band's waltzes, the
radio, the arcade, the café murmur). They are original and need no licence.

Three field recordings stand in where a recording sounds better than synthesis. Each is a Freesound
sound whose own page gives its licence as **Creative Commons 0** (public domain dedication, commercial
use without attribution). Checked on 2026-10-25. Credited here anyway, with thanks.

| File (site/static/assets/town/sound/) | Source | Author | Used for | What was done |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| sea_gentle.webm / .m4a | https://freesound.org/people/bruno.auzet/sounds/838025/ (lapping on a concrete pier, Le Conquet) | bruno.auzet | water lapping below the railing | 21 s loop from 48.0–70.5 s, as above |
